IntoTheLighthouse will be a calm place to reach a real person for a focused conversation when things feel heavy. Click through each step below. Nothing you type leaves this page.

What the service offers

  • Focused conversations with a practitioner, for difficult moments.
  • Two ways in: book a time that suits you, or ask for the next available time.
  • Only what's needed: a name and a way to contact you. You don't have to explain everything.
  • Not an emergency service. If a life is at risk, call your local emergency number. See emergency numbers and crisis lines.
