If you or someone else is in danger right now

⌄

IntoTheLighthouse is not an emergency or 24/7 crisis service. If a life is at risk, please call your local emergency number now, or go to your nearest emergency department.

  • UK999 or 112
  • Ireland112 or 999
  • United States and Canada911
  • Australia000

What IntoTheLighthouse is, and isn't

⌄

IntoTheLighthouse offers focused, human conversations for difficult moments: a calm place to be heard and to find your next step.

It is not an emergency service, it is not available around the clock, and it does not replace medical care, diagnosis or treatment. If you are already working with a doctor or mental health professional, please keep in touch with them too.

Who we support. Adults are welcome. Young people under 18 are welcome too, and are supported only by practitioners who hold the legal qualifications to work with under-18s.

◇

Keeping you safe

⌄

What you share in a conversation stays between you and the practitioner.

There is one exception. If we believe that you, or someone else, especially a child, is at serious risk of harm, we may need to contact emergency or safeguarding services. Wherever it is safe to do so, we will talk with you about it first.

◷

Your privacy

⌄

Right now, nothing you type here is collected. The request form is not connected yet, so it does not send or store anything. This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to display the text.

Before bookings or requests open, this page will set out plainly what we collect and why, where it is kept and who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to ask for it to be deleted. We will only ask for what is needed to contact you, and we will never sell your details or use them for marketing.

Reviews. If you would like to leave a review of your experience, it is warmly welcome. That is always your own invitation to make: we won't ask you for one or encourage it.

These details were last reviewed in November 2026. Phone numbers can change; if one doesn't connect, findahelpline.com keeps an up-to-date list.
